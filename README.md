### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer**, **Co-founder & CTO at KENOL Tech**, **Yango STEM Fellow**, and Computer Science student at **Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU)** based in Accra, Ghana.

I build scalable, high-impact systems for African tech ecosystems — from cloud platforms and hosting infrastructure to educational tools that democratize learning. My work bridges **full-stack engineering**, **DevOps**, and **product strategy** to ship production-grade solutions that solve real problems.

* 🚀 **Current Focus**: Architecting [KENOL Cloud](https://kenol.cloud) & federated institutional tools.
* 🏆 **Fellowship**: Yango STEM Fellow championing digital transformation and tech education across Africa.
* 💬 **Ask me about**: Cloud infrastructure, Go, Vue.js, Docker, NestJS, distributed architectures & SaaS development.
* ⚡ **Fun Fact**: Built a full-fledged top-down space shooter game in Java & Processing!
